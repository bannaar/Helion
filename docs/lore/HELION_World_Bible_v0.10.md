## 1. SETTING OVERVIEW

### HUMANITY DID NOT CONQUER THE STARS. IT INHERITED A CRIME SCENE.

Helion takes place several centuries after humanity spread through a network of naturally occurring and artificially stabilized transit phenomena called Helion Gates.

Nobody knows who originally built the oldest portions of the network. Humanity merely learned how to turn it back on.

That discovery produced the **Expansion Age**, when governments, corporations, religious movements, prospectors, and entire populations poured outward. Colonies became nations. Mining settlements became industrial powers. Corporate security fleets became navies.

Then came the **Severance**.

A large section of the gate network failed almost simultaneously. Systems vanished from contact. Trade collapsed. Some colonies starved. Others became completely independent civilizations.

Something also began appearing around the dead gates.

It was not human.

The present era is roughly a century later. Civilization has recovered, but there is no unified galactic government. Inhabited space is a patchwork of powers held together by commerce, treaties, private industry, and the surviving gate network.

The player begins in one of the comparatively quiet frontier systems: **Kepler**.

Quiet does not last.

## 3. MAJOR HUMAN POWERS

### THE HELION COMMONWEALTH

**Philosophy:** "Civilization survives because somebody keeps the lights on."

**Overview:** The largest successor to the old interstellar government. Controls wealthy core systems and much of the surviving gate infrastructure.

**Government:** Federal parliamentary republic with an Assembly of Systems.

**Capital:** Concordia

**Strengths:** Stability, infrastructure, scientific institutions, large professional navy.

**Weaknesses:** Bureaucracy, political infighting, dependence on corporations.

**Gameplay Identity:**
- Lawful and relatively secure space
- Beginner-friendly systems
- Exploration and scientific missions
- Rescue and logistics
- Military contracts
- Anti-piracy operations
- Restricted technology unlocked through reputation

**Reputation Gains:** Patrols, rescues, delivery of critical supplies, bounty hunting, exploration, military service.

**Criminal Activity:** Produces Commonwealth wanted status; patrols scan, interdict, fine, arrest, or attack depending on severity.

---

### THE SOLAR DIRECTORATE

**Philosophy:** "Survival requires coordination before consensus."

**Overview:** Arose during the Severance when fragmented emergency governments could not coordinate. Military and logistics commands assumed emergency powers and never fully surrendered that authority.

**Government:** Centralized technocratic state led by a Director-General and Strategic Council.

**Strengths:** Low violent crime, excellent infrastructure, strong medical services, efficient disaster response.

**Weaknesses:** Pervasive surveillance, extensive regulation, limited personal freedom.

**Gameplay Identity:**
- Military careers
- Restricted technology
- Security checkpoints
- Cargo inspections
- Smuggling
- Espionage
- Political missions
- Transponder and stealth gameplay

**Security Classifications:** Cargo classified as legal, restricted, or prohibited. Hidden cargo compartments, forged documentation, stealth systems, bribery, and alternative routes become valuable.

---

### THE FREE SYSTEMS COMPACT

**Philosophy:** "Nobody gets to abandon its worlds twice."

**Overview:** Formed from colonies abandoned during the Severance. Individual systems retain enormous autonomy.

**Government:** Mutual-defense and trade association with a deliberately weak central government and rotating Conclave.

**Engineering Philosophy:** "If it cannot be repaired in a hangar with ordinary tools, somebody designed it incorrectly."

**Gameplay Identity:**
- Frontier settlements
- Independent mining
- Salvage
- Bounty hunting
- Local militias
- Player organizations
- System-level politics
- Rugged modular ships

**Reputation Layers Example:**
- Commonwealth: FRIENDLY
- Free Compact: ALLIED
- Kepler Authority: REVERED
- Orion Extraction Group: NEUTRAL
- Vanta Syndicate: HOSTILE

---

### THE MERIDIAN LEAGUE

**Philosophy:** "The market decides."

**Overview:** Less a conventional country than a civilization organized around commerce. Systems governed through a federation of trading houses, station governments, corporations, and commercial interests.

**Government:** Citizen Chamber, Systems Chamber, and Commercial Chamber.

**Gameplay Identity:**
- Trading
- Hauling
- Commodity speculation
- Corporate contracts
- Industrial competition
- Smuggling
- Economic warfare
- Major shipyards and exchanges

**Dynamic Events:** Wars increase demand for metals, fuel, weapons, and medicine. Famines increase food prices. Construction projects consume machinery. Pirate activity makes safe trade routes more valuable.

---

### THE OUTER REACH

**Overview:** Not a unified government. Everything beyond reliable government control.

**Includes:** Mining colonies, failed terraforming projects, independent stations, scientific enclaves, religious communities, smuggler ports, pirate kingdoms, forgotten settlements.

**Security Gradient:**
Commonwealth Navy → System Security → Private Security → Local Militia → No Effective Authority

**Notable Systems:**

- **Harrow** (Agricultural World)
- **Vantage** (Industrial System)
- **Crossroads** (Major Trade Junction)
- **Farside** (Mining System)

### THE REACH

**Characteristics:** No unified authority, independent settlements, prospectors and miners, pirate activity, scientific enclaves, religious communities, smuggler ports, forgotten colonies, unexplained phenomena.

**Security Gradient:**

Every charted human system has a **System Security Rating** from **-5.0 to +5.0**, changing only in **0.2 increments**.

- **+0.2 to +5.0:** High Security. Major populated systems and established national territory.
- **0.0:** Zero Space. Unclaimed, uncontrolled space beyond the dependable borders of Low Security.
- **-0.2 to -5.0:** Low Security. Borderlands, fringe systems, hazardous routes, lesser-traveled gates, pirate corridors, anomaly-rich regions, and weakly governed territory.

The detailed security-response model is defined in **Section 36: System Security, Jurisdiction & Frontier Space**.

### CONNECTOR GATES

In addition to major hubs and strategically important Helion Gates, inhabited space is threaded together by **smaller Connector Gates** used for routine system-to-system travel.

Connector Gates are especially important to:

- local trade
- commuter and civilian shipping
- mining routes
- military logistics
- border patrol
- smuggling
- piracy
- exploration toward the frontier

Inside High Security, Connector Gates are normally well charted, monitored, maintained, and surrounded by regular civilian and security traffic.

Toward the Low Security frontier, Connector Gates become less traveled and less consistently protected. Maintenance, traffic-control coverage, patrol frequency, and nearby infrastructure decline.

Beyond the dependable frontier, toward **0.0 Zero Space**, gates and route behavior become increasingly strange. Charts become incomplete, traffic falls away, unusual gate conditions appear, and some routes lead into systems containing anomalies, rare asteroid fields, unexplained phenomena, or poorly understood infrastructure.

The deeper meaning of unusual frontier-gate behavior remains unresolved.

**Notable Locations:**
- **Port Prosperity** (Independent Station)
- **The Shroud** (Nebula Region)
- **New Providence** (Religious Colony)
- **Last Light** (Failing Terraforming Project)
- **The Verge** (Rift Detection Region)

### THE HELION CONCORDAT

**Founding Principle:** "No flag owns the road between stars."

**Overview:** The Helion Concordat is an independent, extraterritorial interstellar institution created after the Severance to protect civilization in places where national jurisdiction, national intelligence, or national military power cannot act quickly enough.

The Concordat is **not a government**.

It claims no civilian population, does not govern ordinary planetary life, does not participate in elections, and does not exist to replace the sovereign nations of Known Space.

It exists because interstellar threats do not reliably stop at borders.

The Concordat operates through three coequal arms:

- **INSA:** Intelligence, counterintelligence, covert investigation, threat analysis, and strategic warning
- **Gatewatch:** Gate science, advanced technology, anomaly observation, technical protection, and classified research
- **Galactic Defense Force:** Large-scale naval protection, route security, rescue, defense, and military response

A common Concordat saying summarizes the relationship:

**INSA knows. Gatewatch understands. The GDF acts.**

None of the three arms commands the other two as a permanent subordinate service. They cooperate under the Crossroads Charter and may form joint commands for defined threats.

### POLITICAL & LEGAL INDEPENDENCE

The Concordat is politically and legally subordinate to **no external sovereign power**.

Its ships, officers, installations, intelligence services, scientific commands, and operational decisions are governed by the **Crossroads Charter** and Concordat law rather than by the domestic chain of command of any nation.

Recognized powers may cooperate with the Concordat, negotiate with it, deny voluntary assistance, or protest its actions, but no nation can legally:

- order the Concordat into a national war
- recall Concordat officers because of their birth nationality
- nationalize a Concordat fleet or installation
- compel INSA to surrender intelligence for partisan purposes
- compel Gatewatch to release restricted Gate technology
- command the GDF to attack a political rival
- use the Concordat to decide elections or domestic political disputes

Concordat independence does **not** mean absence of law. The institution is bound internally by its Charter, its own courts and tribunals, inspectorates, rules of engagement, scientific safety protocols, and command accountability.

### THE CROSSROADS CHARTER

The Crossroads Charter, ratified in **222 AS**, formally joined INSA, Gatewatch, and the GDF under the Helion Concordat banner while preserving the operational independence of each arm.

The Charter established several enduring principles:

1. **Neutrality:** The Concordat does not belong to a nation or political faction.
2. **Human Protection:** Protection of civilian life and lawful interstellar movement is a core duty.
3. **Gate Stewardship:** No single nation may monopolize existential Gate knowledge held in trust by Gatewatch.
4. **Independent Intelligence:** INSA may investigate transnational threats without serving as a national intelligence proxy.
5. **Independent Force:** The GDF may act across political borders within its Charter mandate.
6. **No Territorial Ambition:** Concordat forces cannot conquer territory for political rule.
7. **Internal Accountability:** Extraordinary independence requires extraordinary auditing, review, and command responsibility.

### CONCORDAT BANNER & INSIGNIA

The Concordat's common insignia is a **broken Gate-ring surrounding three equal stars**.

- The broken ring represents the Severance and the fragility of interstellar civilization.
- The three equal stars represent INSA, Gatewatch, and the GDF.
- No star is placed above the others.

The design appears on joint facilities, diplomatic credentials, rescue beacons, Concordat stations, and vessels operating under unified command.

Individual arms retain their own insignia, uniforms, and fleet markings.

### CONCORDAT GOVERNANCE

The Concordat is administered by a small central Secretariat and Charter institutions rather than a civilian parliament.

Core governance includes:

- the senior leadership of INSA, Gatewatch, and the GDF
- a Charter Secretariat handling common administration
- a Concordat Inspectorate responsible for internal investigations and misuse of authority
- Charter tribunals for major internal legal matters
- joint threat councils assembled when intelligence, Gate science, and military action overlap

The Concordat deliberately has no national delegation chamber. National governments communicate with it diplomatically rather than occupying command seats within it.

### INDEPENDENT FINANCING

To reduce political leverage by individual governments, Concordat funding is diversified and structurally separated from any one nation.

Funding may include:

- irrevocable founding endowments established under the Charter
- service and transit assessments attached to Concordat-protected facilities and routes
- international infrastructure agreements
- salvage and legally seized pirate assets where permitted by Charter law
- voluntary contributions that do not purchase command authority
- long-term commercial and scientific service contracts

No donor purchases operational control.

### INTERSTELLAR NAVIGATION & SECURITY AUTHORITY (INSA)

**Identity:** Intelligence arm of the Helion Concordat.

INSA is not ordinary police. It is the Concordat's strategic intelligence, counterintelligence, covert investigation, and threat-analysis service.

INSA exists because the Blind Corridor Years proved that a threat can exploit the gaps between national intelligence systems faster than governments can share information.

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Strategic intelligence
- Signals intelligence
- Counterintelligence
- Piracy-network analysis
- Organized-crime intelligence
- Counter-smuggling intelligence
- Espionage and hostile-service analysis
- Terrorism and sabotage investigations
- Gate-sabotage intelligence
- Stolen military or classified technology investigations
- Illegal alien-technology trafficking intelligence
- Threat assessment for the GDF and Gatewatch
- Covert observation and infiltration
- Identification of transnational hostile networks

**INSA Fleet:** INSA maintains a capable but specialized fleet rather than a conventional battle navy.

Typical INSA vessels include:
- stealth reconnaissance ships
- signals-intelligence platforms
- surveillance corvettes and frigates
- covert transports
- fast courier vessels
- electronic-intelligence ships
- rapid retrieval craft
- specialized boarding and evidence-recovery vessels

INSA normally wins by knowing where the threat is, what it intends to do, and where it will be next.

### BLACKSTAR MARSHALS

The **Blackstar Marshals** are INSA's elite field enforcement, retrieval, and counterterrorist arm.

They are used for:
- high-risk fugitive retrieval
- capture of major pirate or terrorist leaders
- classified boarding actions
- intelligence recovery
- protection or extraction of critical witnesses
- recovery of stolen strategic technology
- emergency action against threats too mobile for ordinary jurisdiction

Blackstars are not beat police and should not routinely replace local security.

### INTERSTELLAR CRIMINAL ESCALATION

A severe criminal case may progress through increasingly broad jurisdiction:

**Local Fine -> System Warrant -> Faction Bounty -> Interstellar Warrant -> Blackstar Directive (rare)**

INSA normally provides the intelligence, correlation, tracking, and threat picture behind high-level interstellar warrants. The Blackstar Marshals are one possible enforcement instrument for exceptional cases.

### CONCORDAT CLASSIFIED TECHNOLOGY

The GDF's technological advantage comes from the unusual relationship between the three Concordat arms.

**INSA contributes:**
- captured hostile technology
- threat signatures
- signals intelligence
- encryption and cryptanalysis
- stealth detection intelligence
- hostile doctrine
- classified targeting and threat data

**Gatewatch contributes:**
- exotic sensors
- Gate-field research
- gravitic instrumentation
- anomaly detection
- experimental power systems
- advanced navigation
- alien-material research
- prototype defensive technology

**The GDF operationalizes selected systems** into deployable military technology.

This can produce superior:
- ECM
- ECCM
- hardened communications
- fleet coordination
- sensor suites
- classified countermeasures
- specialized navigation systems
- experimental defensive systems

Not every GDF vessel carries classified equipment. The most advanced systems are concentrated on command ships, strategic-response groups, expeditionary fleets, Gate-response units, reconnaissance platforms, and special operations formations.

### THE THREE-KEY DOCTRINE

Some technologies are considered too dangerous for a single Concordat arm to control alone.

Under the **Three-Key Doctrine**, selected classified systems require three forms of authorization:

- **INSA:** Threat and intelligence justification
- **Gatewatch:** Technical and scientific authorization
- **GDF:** Operational authorization

This doctrine exists to prevent any single branch from combining unrestricted intelligence, dangerous experimental science, and overwhelming military force without internal checks.

### FOUNDING & MAJOR CONCORDAT EVENTS

#### 187 AS - THE SEVERANCE

The Gate network collapses. Surviving Gate scientists, engineers, technicians, and military specialists remain at functioning installations even as governments fragment.

This loose emergency community becomes known simply as **the Watch**.

#### 188-194 AS - THE FIRST WATCHES

Independent Gate teams begin exchanging telemetry, transit failures, anomalous energy readings, and unexplained sightings outside normal national channels.

The cooperation is technically illegal in several successor states, but the teams continue because Gate failures do not respect borders.

These networks become the cultural origin of Gatewatch.

#### 196 AS - THE LANTERN PROTOCOL

An unstable Gate begins cycling without a transit request. Multiple governments arrive with incompatible orders and mutually restricted data.

Independent Gate specialists force a joint investigation.

The resulting **Lantern Protocol** establishes protected scientific cooperation around anomalous Gate events and is treated as Gatewatch's first formal charter.

#### 201-205 AS - THE BLIND CORRIDOR YEARS

Reopened trade routes are exploited by increasingly sophisticated pirate networks using forged transponders, compromised officials, stolen navigation data, infiltrated stations, and advance knowledge of military movements.

National intelligence agencies each hold fragments of the picture but share too little, too slowly.

#### 205 AS - THE NINE BLACK CONVOYS

Nine civilian convoys vanish across four political jurisdictions in eleven weeks.

Investigators eventually prove that one transnational network orchestrated the attacks and survived because intelligence was trapped behind political borders.

Public outrage forces creation of a shared intelligence clearinghouse.

#### 207 AS - INSA IS FORMED

The Interstellar Navigation & Security Authority begins as a shared intelligence service for piracy, organized crime, sabotage, and threats to interstellar transit.

Its supposedly temporary mission rapidly becomes permanent.

#### 212 AS - THE ARCTURUS RETRIEVAL

INSA locates the architect of a major Gate-sabotage network repeatedly moving through disputed jurisdictions.

When no government will authorize a timely cross-border operation, an INSA field team retrieves the target independently.

The operation becomes the precedent for the **Blackstar Marshals**.

#### 218 AS - THE CROSSROADS MASSACRE

A large civilian convoy near Crossroads is attacked by a pirate coalition while several national fleets remain close enough to intervene.

Political boundaries and competing authorization procedures delay action.

By the time formal permission is resolved, the convoy has been devastated.

Memorials across Known Space carry the phrase:

**THEY DIED WAITING FOR PERMISSION.**

The disaster becomes the moral foundation of the GDF.

#### 219 AS - THE GALACTIC DEFENSE FORCE IS FORMED

The GDF is established with one extraordinary condition: **no national government may command it.**

Its mandate is to protect civilian life, interstellar infrastructure, and lawful movement where ordinary national forces are constrained by borders or politics.

#### 222 AS - THE CROSSROADS CHARTER

INSA, Gatewatch, and the GDF enter a common institutional framework: the **Helion Concordat**.

The Charter establishes the three as coequal arms and formally protects the Concordat from national command.

#### 226 AS - THE SEVERED FLAG CRISIS

During a diplomatic crisis, the Solar Directorate attempts to recall Directorate-born officers serving in the GDF.

The Concordat refuses. Many officers refuse as well.

The confrontation nearly breaks the new institution, but establishes the **Severed Flag Principle**: a Concordat commission cannot be subordinated to a command from the officer's former national chain of authority.

#### 231 AS - OPERATION OPEN ROAD

A pirate confederation seizes several Connector Gate chains and extorts civilian commerce.

INSA maps the pirate organization. Gatewatch determines safe transit patterns and supplies advanced navigation support. The GDF deploys a full expeditionary force and reopens the routes.

Operation Open Road becomes the classic example of the three arms operating as one system.

#### 238 AS - THE THREE-KEY DOCTRINE

Gatewatch develops technology with profound sensor and electronic-warfare implications. INSA recognizes its intelligence value; the GDF sees its battlefield potential.

The Concordat responds by creating the Three-Key Doctrine for selected classified technologies.

#### 244 AS - THE BLACK GLASS INCIDENT

Gatewatch detects an unknown object emerging from a damaged Gate. INSA imposes an intelligence quarantine after intercepting evidence that multiple governments and private groups are attempting to obtain it. The GDF establishes an exclusion zone while Gatewatch conducts recovery.

The official report describes the object as a destroyed navigation hazard.

Rumors later connect the incident to advances in Concordat sensor technology. The truth remains classified.

#### 252 AS - THE MERIDIAN RELIEF CRISIS

A colonial disaster leaves a large civilian population dependent on emergency shipping while governments dispute customs authority and access.

The GDF declares a humanitarian corridor and moves relief traffic through the disputed route.

The incident establishes the Concordat's **Right of Humanitarian Passage** doctrine and becomes both a celebrated rescue and a lasting source of political resentment.

#### 263-264 AS - THE SILENT WAR

INSA uncovers a coordinated effort to infiltrate Gatewatch research teams, GDF logistics, shipyards, and strategic communications.

For nearly two years, arrests, disappearances, intercepted transmissions, compromised facilities, and unexplained ship losses occur across Known Space.

No enemy is publicly identified.

Inside the Concordat, the period is known as **the Silent War**.

#### 271 AS - THE BATTLE OF THE VESPER CHAIN

A pirate coalition large enough to function as a minor state occupies several Low Security systems.

The GDF deploys a complete fleet group including reconnaissance, ECM/ECCM, carriers, heavy combat ships, escorts, tankers, repair ships, medical vessels, and logistics support.

The campaign proves that the GDF is not merely a patrol service but a true full-spectrum navy capable of sustained independent warfighting.

#### 279 AS - THE VERGE SIGNAL

Gatewatch detects an impossible transmission near the Verge. INSA confirms that unrelated groups have received fragments of the same signal. A GDF task force quietly establishes a perimeter.

The operation remains classified and becomes one of the threads leading toward the later alien and Unknown Space story.

## 10. ALIEN DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Helion's alien storyline is built around two major extraterrestrial civilizations and the hidden region called Unknown Space.

**The Khepri:** Humanity's living first-contact mystery. Biologically and technologically alien, can enter Known Space at will, not initially hostile.

**The Vael:** Ancient and deeply mysterious post-biological civilization. Humanity first encounters their ruins, autonomous guardians, dormant installations, and advanced technology.

**The Choir:** Collective consciousness that opposes the Null. Exists at the consciousness layer of reality.

**The Null:** The absence of existence. The enemy beyond. The reason the Khepri migrate.

**The Architects:** The original gate builders. Unknown. Ancient. Gone.

## 11. RIFTS

### OVERVIEW

Rifts are unstable spacetime passages connecting Known Space to Unknown Space and sometimes connecting regions of Unknown Space to one another.

Unlike Helion Gates, Rifts have no permanent gate structure and normally cannot be selected directly from a navigation map.

### DETECTION SEQUENCE

System Scan → Detect Unknown Gravitational Signature → Launch Probes → Triangulate → Resolve Signature → Identify Rift → Bookmark → Enter

### RIFT PROPERTIES

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Stability | How reliable transit is |
| Remaining Lifetime | How long before collapse |
| Maximum Ship Mass | Largest ship that can transit |
| Total Transit Mass | Total mass allowed through |
| Destination Class | Where the Rift leads |
| Signature Strength | How detectable it is |
| Environmental Effects | Hazards during transit |
| Return-Route Uncertainty | Whether return is possible |

### RIFT TYPES

**Standard Rifts:** Visible distortion. Stable enough for transit. Variable lifetime.

**Black Rifts:** Perfectly black. Probes lose contact. Vael systems refuse to calculate. Khepri avoid. Extremely dangerous.

**Deep Rifts:** Connect to Deep Unknown Space. Rare. Unstable. Potentially one-way.

**Micro Rifts:** Too small for ships. Useful for probes.

**Unstable Rifts:** Collapsing. Dangerous transit. Stranding risk.

## 12. UNKNOWN SPACE

### OVERVIEW

Unknown Space is not represented on normal human navigation charts.

**Characteristics:**
- No conventional GalNet infrastructure
- No guaranteed navigation beacons
- Few or no human stations
- Uncertain rescue capability
- Unstable routes
- Alien structures
- Ancient wreckage
- Unmapped resources
- Rare technologies
- High-risk exploration sites

### REGIONS

**The Shallows:** Closest to Known Space. Most explored. Khepri activity. Vael ruins. Relatively safe.

**The Middle Depths:** Further from Known Space. Less explored. More dangerous. Ancient structures.

**The Deep:** Far from Known Space. Barely explored. Extremely dangerous. Major structures. Ultimate mysteries.

**The Veil:** Vael distributed consciousness. Information space.

**The Null:** Source of threat. Khepri avoidance. Ultimate danger.

### DISCOVERABLE SIGNATURES

- Additional Rifts
- Alien vessels
- Vael ruins
- Dormant stations
- Ancient fleets
- Resource fields
- Anomalies
- Artificial signals
- Khepri activity
- Unknown high-threat signatures

## 13. THE KHEPRI

### OVERVIEW

The Khepri are humanity's first major encounter with a living extraterrestrial civilization.

They are not initially hostile.

Humanity does not discover the Khepri in their home territory.

The Khepri discover humanity.

### FIRST CONTACT

The first documented Khepri encounter occurs when an unidentified vessel approaches a human ship operating near the frontier.

**Vessel Characteristics:**
- No human transponder
- No recognizable drive plume
- No known reactor signature
- No response to conventional communication

It does not attack. It approaches, scans the human vessel, observes its activity, and leaves.

### BIOLOGY

| Characteristic | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Cellular Structure | Organic and mineral components |
| Nervous System | Distributed neural networks |
| Metabolism | Multiple energy sources |
| Reproduction | Unknown |
| Lifespan | Unknown (possibly centuries or millennia) |
| Communication | Chemical, electromagnetic, physical, Rift resonance |

### VESSEL CASTES

**Observer:** Small reconnaissance organism. Approaches human ships, scans, watches, avoids combat, withdraws when threatened.

**Gatherer:** Resource-collection organism. Mines, harvests, collects wreckage, investigates cargo.

**Shepherd:** Coordination organism. Directs groups, protects Gatherers and Observers, demonstrates complex communication.

**Guardian:** Defensive combat organism. Appears after repeated aggression, protects Khepri vessels and sites.

**Reaver:** Specialized combat organism. Appears after prolonged conflict, uses adapted tactics, hunts known hostile targets.

**Broodship:** Massive mobile Khepri organism capable of supporting or producing smaller castes.

### DISPOSITION SYSTEM

| State | Trigger | Behavior |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Curious | Default | Approach, observe |
| Observing | Sustained contact | Maintain distance, watch |
| Cautious | Unusual behavior | Withdraw, monitor |
| Defensive | Threat perceived | Protective posture |
| Agitated | Aggression experienced | Warning behavior |
| Hostile | Attack suffered | Retaliate |
| Hunting | Sustained conflict | Pursue, destroy |

### ADAPTATION

Khepri can learn collectively. Repeated use of particular human weapons or tactics can alter future encounters.

**Possible Adaptations:**
- Armor resistance
- Different engagement ranges
- New defensive behavior
- Countermeasures
- New combat castes

### KHEPRI GAMEPLAY CAREERS

- **Xenobiology:** Collect biological and technological samples
- **Xenolinguistics:** Analyze signals and develop communication models
- **Exploration:** Locate Khepri structures and activity
- **Trade:** Discover materials Khepri value
- **Diplomacy:** Participate in controlled contact experiments
- **Combat:** Fight hostile Khepri when relations deteriorate
- **Salvage:** Recover components from destroyed alien vessels

## 14. THE VAEL

### OVERVIEW

The Vael are an ancient, extremely advanced civilization associated with Unknown Space.

Humanity initially believes they are extinct.

### FIRST ENCOUNTERS

Human explorers first encounter:
- Vael ruins
- Dormant stations
- Artificial moons
- Ancient fleets
- Automated industrial complexes
- Data archives
- Defensive systems
- Autonomous guardians

Many structures are thousands or millions of years old. Some remain operational.

### THE SENTINELS

Humanity initially calls the autonomous defenders of Vael sites Sentinels.

Sentinels normally ignore or observe explorers who do not interfere with protected structures.

They become defensive when players:
- Damage Vael structures
- Remove protected technology
- Attack Sentinels
- Attempt restricted access
- Activate dangerous systems

### EXPLORATION SITES

**Vael Archive:** Ancient information repository. Scanning, decoding, hacking, historical discoveries.

**Dormant Citadel:** Large fortified structure with automated defenses.

**Silent Fleet:** Hundreds of ancient vessels drifting in formation.

**Machine Garden:** Autonomous manufacturing ecosystem.

**The Observatory:** Ancient installation pointed toward regions beyond mapped Unknown Space.

**Grave System:** System filled with destroyed Vael structures and vessels.

### VAEL FACTIONS

**The Custodians:** Protect Vael ruins, history, and technology. Generally opposed to uncontrolled human salvage.

**The Awakened:** Return to active physical civilization and engage with the changing galaxy. Curious and potentially cooperative.

**The Continuum:** Remain primarily within the Veil. Distant. Human biological civilization may appear primitive.

**The Exiles:** Vael who rejected or abandoned the Veil. Attitude toward humanity varies widely.

**The Severed:** Damaged or corrupted Vael population associated with an ancient catastrophe.

### THE VEIL

Much of Vael civilization transitioned into enormous distributed computational environments collectively called the Veil.

For many Vael, physical bodies became optional. Some remained biological. Some became synthetic. Some exist primarily as information.

### VAEL TECHNOLOGY

**Recoverable Materials:**
- Vael Power Matrix
- Vael Sensor Core
- Unknown Neural Substrate
- Phase Conduit
- Spacetime Regulator
- Ancient Navigation Core

**Legal Status:** Corporations pay for them. Gatewatch may restrict them. Governments may classify them. Smugglers may traffic them.

## 15. THE CHOIR

### OVERVIEW

The Choir is a collective consciousness. Individual minds remain distinct but connected. They share thoughts, memories, emotions, and experiences.

### NATURE

The Choir is not:
- A hive mind (individuality is preserved)
- A single being (many minds exist)
- A physical entity (they exist in consciousness)
- A technological construct (they are natural)

The Choir is:
- A collective
- A harmony
- A presence
- A mystery

### COMMUNICATION

The Choir experiences reality as music. Their consciousness is harmony. Their communication is melody.

Early attempts to decode Choir communication have produced fragments:
- "We hear you."
- "You are not alone."
- "The song is ending."
- "Listen."

### RELATIONSHIPS

**The Choir and the Architects:** Unknown. The Choir may be the Architects. They may be their descendants. They may be unrelated.

**The Choir and the Khepri:** The Choir may be trying to help the Khepri. The Choir may be trying to warn them.

**The Choir and the Vael:** The Vael know of the Choir. They fear them. They respect them. They do not understand them.

**The Choir and the Null:** The Choir opposes the Null. They are the opposite of the Null.

## 16. THE NULL

### OVERVIEW

The Null is the greatest mystery in Helion. It is the reason the Khepri are migrating. It is what the Vael fear. It is what the Choir opposes.

### NATURE

The Null is not:
- A civilization
- A species
- A natural phenomenon
- A cosmic threat

The Null is:
- An absence
- A negation
- An ending
- A silence

### WHAT IS KNOWN

- Khepri avoid it
- Vael fear it
- Choir opposes it
- Associated with Black Rifts
- Associated with the Severance
- Getting closer

### THE NULL AND THE SEVERANCE

The Severance may be connected to the Null. The gate network failed. The barriers weakened. The Null is closer.

**Evidence:**
- Gate failures preceded by signals
- Anomalous readings
- Khepri activity increased after Severance
- Null activity increased after Severance

---

## 17. THE ARCHITECTS

### OVERVIEW

The Helion Gates were not built by humanity. They were not built by the Vael. They were built by someone else—someone older, more advanced, and completely unknown.

### WHAT IS KNOWN

**Age:** Millions of years old at minimum. Possibly billions.

**Design:** Consistent across vast distances. Same technology. Same architecture. Same principles.

**Function:** Allow faster-than-light travel by creating stable wormholes.

**Network:** Forms a network with structure and purpose.

**Abandonment:** The Architects are gone. Their fate is unknown.

### THEORIES

**The Architects Were Vael:** Disproven. Vael records indicate they found the gates, not built them.

**The Architects Were Choir:** Possible.

**The Architects Were Khepri:** Possible.

**The Architects Were Someone Else:** Most likely.

**The Architects Were The Null:** Possible.

### THE NETWORK'S PURPOSE

**The Gates Are a Computer:** The gate network is a computational device.

**The Gates Are a Sensor:** The gate network is a sensor array.

**The Gates Are a Defense:** The gate network is a defensive system.

**The Gates Are a Prison:** The gate network is a containment system.

## 31. CHARACTER CREATION & NATIONAL ORIGIN

### THE COMMANDER IS A PERSON BEFORE THEY ARE A SHIP.

Character creation establishes the player's human identity, national origin, appearance, cultural presentation, and initial relationship to the inhabited universe.

At character creation, the player chooses a **national origin** from the major human powers available to players.

Initial playable origins:

- Helion Commonwealth
- Solar Directorate
- Free Systems Compact
- Meridian League
- Aurelian Synod

The Outer Reach is a region rather than a unified nation and is not treated as a single nationality. Independent or Reach-born origins may be added later where supported by lore and gameplay.

### NATIONAL ORIGIN

National origin should matter without locking the player into a permanent career or political allegiance.

Origin may influence:

- character biography
- introductory dialogue
- clothing and portrait presets
- cultural styling
- starting contacts
- small initial reputation relationships
- faction-specific tutorial context
- how NPCs recognize or discuss the commander's background
- later military, corporate, political, or cultural opportunities

Origin must **not** permanently determine the player's profession, morality, political loyalty, or future faction relationships.

A Commonwealth-born commander may become a Compact ally. A Directorate citizen may become an independent trader. A Meridian citizen may join Gatewatch. The player's actions shape the person they become.

### AVATAR PORTRAIT CREATION

Every commander has a persistent avatar portrait used throughout HELION.

The portrait should appear where appropriate in:

- launcher/account presentation
- commander profile
- character sheet
- communications
- mission contacts
- station services
- GalNet/community contexts where appropriate
- organization rosters
- multiplayer/social interfaces

The player creates and edits the portrait through a character editor.

Editable appearance should include, where technically supported:

- skin tone
- facial structure
- eyes
- eyebrows
- nose
- mouth
- jaw and chin
- ears
- hair style
- hair color
- facial hair
- age presentation
- scars
- tattoos
- cosmetics
- cybernetic or medical details where lore-appropriate
- clothing
- accessories
- background
- lighting
- pose/expression

The portrait system should support broad human diversity. National identity should be expressed primarily through **culture, styling, grooming, clothing, architecture, insignia, materials, technology, and presentation**, not by restricting a nationality to one ethnicity or skin color.

Centuries of colonization, migration, isolation, founder populations, local environments, and cultural history may produce recognizable population tendencies, but every major human power contains human variation. Governance itself does not biologically determine facial features.

### NATIONAL VISUAL IDENTITIES

Each major power should be visually recognizable in character presentation in the same way its ships, stations, institutions, and technology are recognizable.

#### HELION COMMONWEALTH

**Character Identity:** Cosmopolitan, civic, professional, institutionally diverse.

**Visual Language:**
- practical professional clothing
- naval and civil-service influences
- layered civilian fashion from many member systems
- clean but lived-in presentation
- restrained insignia
- scientific and infrastructure-worker influences

**Population Character:** Highly mixed due to migration between Commonwealth systems. Portrait presets should emphasize diversity rather than a single Commonwealth phenotype.

#### SOLAR DIRECTORATE

**Character Identity:** Ordered, disciplined, technocratic, state-conscious.

**Visual Language:**
- precise grooming
- structured silhouettes
- standardized professional clothing
- geometric insignia
- controlled palettes and presentation
- subtle identification technology
- high-quality but intentionally uniform state design

**Population Character:** Distinctive presentation comes strongly from social standardization, education, military/logistics culture, and regulated aesthetics rather than a single ethnicity.

#### FREE SYSTEMS COMPACT

**Character Identity:** Frontier independence, practicality, local pride, repair culture.

**Visual Language:**
- durable workwear
- modular clothing
- patched or personalized equipment
- local-system insignia
- practical hair and grooming mixed with strong individual expression
- visible occupational wear
- repurposed accessories and technology

**Population Character:** Compact systems were isolated and developed independently after the Severance. Individual systems may have recognizable local communities and founder-population influences, but the Compact as a whole is highly varied.

#### MERIDIAN LEAGUE

**Character Identity:** Commercial confidence, cosmopolitan trade culture, status through taste and success.

**Visual Language:**
- tailored clothing
- corporate and trading-house fashion
- expensive materials
- personal branding
- jewelry and accessories
- polished presentation
- fashionable technological augmentation where appropriate
- visible distinction between working traders, corporate professionals, and wealthy commercial elites

**Population Character:** Centuries of commerce and migration make Meridian populations broadly cosmopolitan. Social class, house affiliation, profession, and fashion are stronger visual signals than ancestry.

#### AURELIAN SYNOD

**Character Identity:** Intellectual, contemplative, scientific, philosophical.

**Visual Language:**
- academic and research clothing
- ceremonial elements derived from the Colleges
- medical/scientific accessories
- subtle symbolic geometry
- observation and cosmology motifs
- understated personal presentation
- distinctive formal wear for College affiliation

**Population Character:** Aurelian identity reflects the history of its isolated colonies and philosophical institutions. Presets may carry recognizable regional traditions while preserving broad player customization.

### CULTURE WITHOUT BIOLOGICAL LOCK-IN

HELION should create recognizable human civilizations without turning nationality into a biological character class.

Therefore:

- no skin tone is faction-exclusive
- no facial structure is faction-exclusive
- no hairstyle is inherently faction-exclusive unless it is a specific cultural/organizational style
- players may customize freely within the human character system
- national presets provide a strong starting identity, not a genetic cage

Distinctiveness should emerge from the combination of:

    HISTORY
        +
    CULTURE
        +
    GOVERNANCE
        +
    ENVIRONMENT
        +
    FASHION
        +
    TECHNOLOGY
        +
    PERSONAL CHOICE

### STARTING LOCATION

National origin does not require five separate opening campaigns.

For the initial game structure, newly licensed independent commanders converge on **Kepler Station**, preserving Kepler as HELION's common starting system and the existing First Ore progression.

The player's origin changes the context of arriving at Kepler, not the fundamental tutorial architecture.

This allows:

- one polished starting experience
- national identity from character creation
- faction-specific dialogue and contacts
- later expansion into alternate starts without rewriting the core progression

### CHARACTER EDITING AFTER CREATION

Players should be able to revise cosmetic appearance after creation through an appropriate character/profile service.

Cosmetic editing should not allow the player to casually rewrite persistent historical facts such as national origin, military history, faction crimes, or earned reputation.

Future systems may provide legitimate citizenship changes, defections, asylum, naturalization, or identity fraud as gameplay rather than a free character-editor toggle.

## 36. SYSTEM SECURITY, JURISDICTION & FRONTIER SPACE

### EVERY SYSTEM HAS A SECURITY RATING.

Every charted human system is assigned a **System Security Rating** ranging from:

    -5.0 to +5.0

Ratings change only in increments of:

    0.2

The security number is not merely a difficulty label. It represents the practical ability and willingness of civilization to enforce law, defend traffic, maintain infrastructure, patrol gates, suppress piracy, and respond to distress.

Security influences:

- local police presence
- faction law enforcement
- Galactic Defense Force presence
- national navy presence
- customs activity
- gate patrols
- response time
- rescue probability
- convoy and escort activity
- NPC mining security
- piracy
- smuggling
- bounty enforcement
- market and shipping risk
- mission generation
- population and infrastructure density
- anomaly and exploration risk

### SECURITY CLASSES

| Rating | Classification | General Character |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| +4.2 to +5.0 | Prime High Security | Capitals, major core systems, extremely dense security and infrastructure |
| +2.2 to +4.0 | High Security | Established national systems, strong law enforcement, frequent patrols |
| +0.2 to +2.0 | Frontier High Security | Recognized national space with regular but thinner protection |
| 0.0 | Zero Space | Unclaimed and uncontrolled; no automatic criminal penalty for player aggression |
| -0.2 to -2.2 | Supported Low Security | Fringe/border systems with limited GDF response for eligible victims |
| -2.4 to -4.0 | Deep Low Security | Weak law; occasional GDF expeditionary anti-piracy activity |
| -4.2 to -5.0 | Extreme Low Security | Remote fringe territory with effectively no dependable outside response |

### HIGH SECURITY: +0.2 TO +5.0

High Security contains the major populated systems of the recognized human nations and the safer territory within their borders.

The higher the rating, the more civilization is visibly present.

High Security may contain:

- dense local police patrols
- faction security forces
- customs patrols
- Galactic Defense Force units
- national navy patrols
- defended stations
- monitored Connector Gates
- emergency rescue assets
- civilian escorts
- NPC industrial operations
- high-volume commercial traffic

#### UNPROVOKED PLAYER AGGRESSION

Attacking another player without a lawful justification in High Security is a criminal act.

Potential consequences include:

- immediate criminal flagging
- local or faction bounty accumulation
- police response
- GDF or faction reinforcement depending on location
- docking or service restrictions
- increased legal consequences for repeated offenses
- escalation to faction or INSA warrants for serious/repeated crime

Security response is not identical at every rating.

A +5.0 core system should feel dramatically more protected than a +0.2 frontier system.

At the highest ratings, attacking innocent traffic should be exceptionally dangerous because multiple layers of security can respond.

### ZERO SPACE: 0.0

**0.0 is a special category.**

Zero Space lies beyond the dependable borders of Low Security and outside recognized routine law enforcement.

It is unknown, unclaimed, or effectively uncontrolled space.

Player aggression in 0.0 receives:

- no automatic local criminal penalty
- no automatic faction criminal bounty
- no automatic GDF criminal bounty
- no guaranteed police rescue
- no guaranteed military intervention

Player organizations, factions, NPC groups, or individual settlements may still react according to their own interests, standings, territory, contracts, or grudges, but 0.0 itself provides no general criminal protection.

Zero Space is the frontier's frontier.

Common characteristics:

- incomplete charts
- strange or poorly understood gate behavior
- rare asteroid fields
- unusual resource concentrations
- anomalies
- derelicts
- unexplained signals
- unknown hazards
- potentially valuable discoveries
- little or no reliable rescue
- high player-versus-player risk
- high exploration and economic opportunity

The risk can be severe.

The reward can be severe.

The central design principle of 0.0 is:

**WHO KNOWS WHAT IS OUT THERE?**

### LOW SECURITY: -0.2 TO -5.0

Low Security consists primarily of outlying systems on the fringes of national borders and the weaker routes between civilization and the unknown.

These systems are often connected by lesser-traveled Connector Gates.

Low Security may contain:

- hazardous navigation
- anomalies
- weak station security
- pirates
- smugglers
- black markets
- isolated industrial operations
- prospectors
- frontier settlements
- criminal organizations
- rare-resource opportunities
- dangerous missions
- reduced infrastructure
- unreliable rescue

Life is rough here, but opportunity grows as protection falls.

### SUPPORTED LOW SECURITY: -0.2 TO -2.2

These are dangerous border systems that remain close enough to recognized civilization for limited interstellar intervention.

If a player who is **Neutral or better** with the relevant authorities is attacked, a **small Galactic Defense Force response** may be dispatched.

Response is not guaranteed to be instantaneous.

Factors may include:

- security rating
- distance from patrol assets
- current GDF workload
- local events
- victim standing
- attacker criminal status
- whether the distress event can be detected

Local security may also respond if it still exists.

The player should feel that help is possible, but not something to casually rely on.

### DEEP LOW SECURITY: -2.4 TO -4.0

Routine rescue response is no longer dependable.

The GDF does not normally function as an on-demand police service here.

However, **Galactic Defense Force expeditionary anti-piracy fleets may occasionally appear**.

These deployments can:

- patrol pirate corridors
- attack known pirate groups
- secure a Connector Gate temporarily
- escort strategic traffic
- respond to major criminal concentrations
- create temporary windows of safer trade
- generate GalNet stories and player opportunities

Their presence should feel like an event, not normal policing.

### EXTREME LOW SECURITY: -4.2 TO -5.0

These systems sit at the ragged edge of claimed civilization.

Organized authorities may exist only at an isolated station, settlement, mining facility, military outpost, or fortified gate.

Players should assume:

- no dependable GDF rescue
- little meaningful local policing
- extremely slow or nonexistent reinforcement
- strong pirate and smuggler activity
- high-value frontier opportunities
- dangerous anomalies and environmental conditions
- heavily armed civilian and industrial traffic

A system may technically belong to a faction while remaining functionally beyond its day-to-day control.

### FACTION SECURITY

Faction Security means the security forces operated by the sovereign power or local political authority that claims a system.

This can include:

- Commonwealth Marshals
- Directorate Internal Security
- Compact local militias and authorities
- Meridian commercial enforcement
- system police
- border patrol
- customs
- national naval detachments

Faction Security is strongest inside that faction's own established territory.

Its behavior may be affected by:

- player faction standing
- legal status
- military rank
- warrants
- local politics
- war
- emergency conditions

### GALACTIC SECURITY

The cross-border security layer is centered on the independent **Helion Concordat**, whose three coequal arms contribute different capabilities:

**INSA - Intelligence**
- strategic intelligence
- counterintelligence
- organized-crime and piracy-network analysis
- covert investigation
- threat warning
- intelligence support for interstellar warrants and Blackstar operations

**Gatewatch - Science & Technology**
- Gate observation and protection
- anomaly analysis
- advanced sensor and technical support
- classified Gate research
- scientific assessment of unknown threats

**Galactic Defense Force - Military Power**
- patrol
- emergency assistance
- anti-piracy response
- convoy support
- inter-system route security
- humanitarian response
- expeditionary and major fleet operations

The Concordat is politically and legally subordinate to no external sovereign. It operates under the Crossroads Charter and its own internal law, while national police and navies retain authority over normal domestic matters.

This structure allows local law, national sovereignty, and genuinely independent interstellar protection to coexist without turning the Concordat into a galactic government.

### NAVY PRESENCE

National navies are not ordinary police.

Their presence increases with:

- strategic importance
- national borders
- military installations
- war
- piracy
- major trade routes
- Gate importance
- emergency events

High Security core systems may have frequent visible navy traffic.

Low Security border systems may contain patrols or forward detachments but cannot rely on continuous naval protection.

### NPC MINING & INDUSTRIAL TRAFFIC

NPC mining may occur throughout both High Security and Low Security.

In High Security:

- mining operations may rely on local police, corporate security, or nearby faction patrols
- escorts vary with system rating and cargo value
- routine civilian mining traffic should help make populated systems feel economically alive

In Low Security:

- NPC mining operations should normally use **armed escorts**
- mining ships may travel in defended groups
- corporate security may be heavily armed
- anti-piracy contracts may appear around industrial sites
- pirates may attack NPC mining operations
- players may defend, raid, salvage, trade with, or otherwise interact with the resulting activity where gameplay systems allow

Industrial traffic should therefore help communicate the security rating without requiring the player to stare at a number.

### DYNAMIC SECURITY RATINGS

Security ratings may change over time, but only in **0.2 increments**.

Possible causes include:

- sustained pirate activity
- player crime
- successful anti-piracy campaigns
- faction investment
- construction of security infrastructure
- war
- loss of stations or patrol bases
- Gate disruption
- economic collapse
- population changes
- major GalNet events
- successful GDF operations
- prolonged instability

Security should not fluctuate randomly from moment to moment.

A rating change should represent a meaningful change in the system's actual condition.

### SECURITY SHOULD BE FELT

A player entering a system should be able to sense its security level through the world itself.

Possible signals include:

- number of patrol vessels
- police chatter
- gate traffic
- customs scans
- navy presence
- armed escorts
- station condition
- pirate activity
- abandoned infrastructure
- navigation warnings
- convoy density
- distress signals
- GalNet reports
- visible anomalies

The numeric rating summarizes the condition.

The world should make the number believable.

## 37. GALACTIC ECONOMY, INDUSTRY & MARKETS

### THE ECONOMY SHOULD CREATE GAMEPLAY.

HELION's economy is part of the living universe rather than a static shop interface.

A shortage should create an opportunity. An opportunity should create movement. Movement should create risk. Risk should create demand for protection, information, equipment, logistics, replacement assets, and new production.

The long-term economic loop is:

    EXTRACTION
        ↓
    REFINING / PROCESSING
        ↓
    COMPONENTS
        ↓
    MANUFACTURING
        ↓
    SHIPS / MODULES / GOODS
        ↓
    REGIONAL MARKETS
        ↓
    USE / CONSUMPTION / DESTRUCTION
        ↓
    RENEWED DEMAND

The intended destination is a strongly player-influenced economy in which player activity can become a major source of resources, manufactured goods, logistics, trade, and market liquidity.

HELION begins with an NPC-seeded economy because a new universe cannot depend on a player industrial base that does not yet exist.

### ECONOMIC DESIGN LAWS

**LORE SHOULD GENERATE ECONOMIC GAMEPLAY.**

Political borders, corporations, wars, piracy, Gate failures, discoveries, colonization, shortages, scientific projects, and alien events should influence economic conditions.

**SCARCITY MUST HAVE A CAUSE.**

Scarcity should emerge from geography, extraction, consumption, destruction, logistics, conflict, events, infrastructure, or player behavior. Difficulty should not exist solely to manufacture arbitrary grind.

**PHYSICAL GOODS HAVE LOCATIONS.**

A commodity in Meridian Prime is not automatically available in Kepler. Geography creates trade.

**INFORMATION MAY TRAVEL FARTHER THAN GOODS.**

Players may eventually learn about remote prices or shortages without teleporting physical inventory.

**PRODUCTION REQUIRES INPUTS.**

Ships, modules, equipment, consumables, and infrastructure should increasingly connect to material supply chains as industrial gameplay develops.

**USE AND DESTRUCTION CREATE DEMAND.**

An economy in which assets only accumulate eventually stagnates. Consumption, damage, destruction, construction, services, and other sinks keep professions relevant.

### CREDITS: FAUCETS, SINKS & TRANSFERS

HELION distinguishes between three forms of credit movement.

**Faucet:** Credits are created by the simulation.

Examples may include:
- mission rewards
- government contracts
- NPC bounties
- selected NPC purchasing programs
- emergency or development incentives

**Sink:** Credits leave circulation.

Examples may include:
- market taxes
- brokerage or listing fees
- repair and service fees
- registration
- licenses and permits
- fines
- manufacturing or facility fees
- insurance-related fees where implemented
- specialized Gate or station services

**Transfer:** Existing credits move between economic participants.

Examples:
- player purchases from another player
- contracts between players
- organization payments
- player market orders

Player-to-player trade normally transfers existing currency rather than creating new currency.

The simulation should track faucets and sinks so inflation, deflation, runaway reward systems, and unhealthy wealth concentration can be detected and balanced.

### COMMODITY TYPES

**Raw Materials:** Ore, ice, gas, organics

**Processed Goods:** Metals, fuel, chemicals, food

**Industrial Components:** Electronics, structural components, drive parts, reactor components, sensor packages, machinery

**Manufactured Goods:** Modules, ships, weapons, equipment, industrial tools

**Consumer Goods:** Food, household goods, medicine, clothing, entertainment, civilian electronics

**Luxury Goods:** Rare materials, art, high-end technology, prestige goods

**Restricted Goods:** Military weapons, controlled substances, prohibited technology, stolen property, alien artifacts where legally restricted

**Alien Materials:** Khepri biological/mineral material, Vael matrices and recovered structures, other xenological materials as discovered

Alien goods must remain consistent with alien lore. The existence of a commodity category does not imply that every alien material is safely tradable, understood, legal, or commercially reproducible.

### RESOURCE EXTRACTION

Extraction is the beginning of many industrial chains.

Sources may include:

- asteroid mining
- ice extraction
- gas harvesting
- planetary or colony production
- salvage
- wreck recovery
- exploration discoveries
- specialized xenological recovery

Resources should vary geographically.

A region rich in one material and poor in another naturally creates hauling and trade.

### REFINING & PROCESSING

Raw materials should increasingly require processing before becoming advanced manufactured goods.

Example:

    ORE
      ↓
    REFINED METAL
      ↓
    STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS
      ↓
    SHIP HULL

Processing creates additional professions, infrastructure requirements, corporate relationships, and regional specialization.

Refining efficiency, access, cost, and capacity may vary by station, corporation, technology, reputation, and region.

### MANUFACTURING

The long-term destination is meaningful player manufacturing.

Potential production includes:

- ammunition and consumables
- mining equipment
- ship modules
- weapons
- electronics
- cargo systems
- industrial components
- ship hulls
- specialized scientific equipment
- organization infrastructure

Manufacturing should require understandable inputs and time/capacity where appropriate.

Future production systems may use blueprints, licenses, manufacturing data, research, reverse engineering, corporate access, or discovered technology, but those mechanics should be developed as HELION systems rather than copied wholesale from another game.

### NPC-SEEDED BOOTSTRAP ECONOMY

At the beginning of HELION's live economy, NPC organizations provide enough liquidity and supply for the universe to function even with a very small player population.

NPC organizations may seed:

- starter equipment
- basic modules
- essential commodities
- fuel and services
- starter and commonly available ships
- buy orders for extracted resources
- sell orders for essential goods
- mission and contract rewards

NPC economic participation should reflect organization identity.

**Orion Extraction Group:** Raw materials, mining equipment, refinery services, industrial contracts.

**Titan Forge:** Heavy industrial equipment, durable modules, military-industrial products.

**Aster Dynamics:** Performance ships, propulsion technology, navigation equipment.

**Horizon Systems:** Sensors, electronics, information and electronic-warfare equipment where implemented.

**Meridian Interests:** Commercial goods, logistics, shipping, trade infrastructure, market liquidity.

**Compact Industry:** Rugged modular equipment, repairable frontier hardware, locally produced goods.

**Helix Interstellar:** Gate-related scientific equipment and specialized restricted technology.

NPC supply is a bootstrap and stability mechanism, not necessarily the permanent dominant source of every item.

As player production and market liquidity become healthy, NPC participation may diminish in appropriate markets while remaining available where required for accessibility, stability, lore, or low-population regions.

### REGIONAL MARKETS

HELION does not have one universal warehouse.

Markets are associated with places.

Relevant layers may include:

- station
- settlement
- system
- region
- faction
- corporation

Prices and availability can differ because of:

- local production
- local consumption
- transportation cost
- security
- taxation
- piracy
- war
- blockades
- Gate disruptions
- infrastructure
- faction policy
- player activity
- world events

A cheap commodity in one system and an expensive commodity elsewhere creates a hauling opportunity.

The same opportunity may create pirate activity.

Piracy may create escort contracts.

Escort demand may increase ship/module demand.

One economic difference should be capable of generating several kinds of gameplay.

### BUY & SELL ORDERS

The long-term market should support actual price discovery.

Market architecture should be capable of supporting:

- NPC buy orders
- NPC sell orders
- player buy orders
- player sell orders

A player may eventually choose between immediate execution against an existing order or placing an order and waiting for another participant.

Market information should communicate useful quantities such as:

- price
- quantity
- location
- recent volume
- historical price where available
- relevant taxes/fees

Physical inventory remains where it is stored until transported through gameplay.

### SECURITY & ECONOMIC RISK

System Security Rating directly affects economic geography.

Higher-security space generally offers:

- stronger protection
- denser infrastructure
- more predictable logistics
- lower transportation risk
- more competition
- stronger customs and legal enforcement

Lower-security space generally offers:

- greater transport risk
- pirate and smuggler activity
- weaker rescue
- armed industrial convoys
- higher security costs
- access to unusual resources and opportunities
- potentially higher margins

Zero Space can contain exceptionally valuable resources and discoveries, but there is no automatic criminal protection for player traffic.

This relationship should create a natural risk/reward gradient without guaranteeing that every Low Security route is more profitable than every High Security route.

### LOGISTICS

Logistics is an economic profession, not merely travel between menus.

Goods must move.

This creates demand for:

- haulers
- cargo ships
- escorts
- route planning
- secure storage
- smuggling
- convoy operations
- blockade running
- intelligence
- insurance or risk-management systems where implemented

Core space may offer safer but more competitive routes.

The Marches may offer developing markets and resource opportunities.

The Reach may offer extreme margins with extreme logistical risk.

### TAXES, FEES & ECONOMIC POLICY

Governments and stations may influence markets through:

- transaction taxes
- listing/brokerage fees
- import/export rules
- tariffs
- docking/service fees
- refining fees
- manufacturing fees
- permits
- subsidies
- embargoes
- contraband laws

These should reinforce political identity.

## 43. TIMELINE OF HUMAN HISTORY

### PRE-EXPANSION

**Before Year 0:** Humanity confined to Sol system. Helion Gates discovered but dormant.

### EXPANSION AGE (0-187)

**Year 0:** Helix Interstellar reactivates first Helion Gate. Expansion begins.

**Year 1-50:** First wave of colonization. Early colonies established.

**Year 50-100:** Great Migration. Population triples. New industries emerge.

**Year 100-150:** Golden Decades. Interstellar Union formed. Trade routes established.

**Year 150-187:** Peak of civilization. 300+ systems. Optimism and growth.

### THE SEVERANCE (187)

**Day 47:** Gate network fails. Cascade effect. 2,400 systems isolated.

**Year 187-190:** Immediate aftermath. Panic, starvation, death.

**Year 190-200:** Successor states form. New powers emerge. Recovery begins.

### POST-SEVERANCE (187-Present)

**Year 187-200:** Dark age. Isolation. Survival.

**Year 188-194:** The First Watches. Independent Gate teams share emergency data across political lines, creating the culture that becomes Gatewatch.

**Year 196:** The Lantern Protocol establishes protected scientific cooperation around anomalous Gate events.

**Year 200-250:** Recovery. Reconnection. New powers.

**Year 201-205:** The Blind Corridor Years. Sophisticated pirate and criminal networks exploit gaps between national intelligence services.

**Year 205:** The Nine Black Convoys disappear across four jurisdictions, triggering demands for a truly cross-border intelligence capability.

**Year 207:** INSA is formally established.

**Year 212:** The Arcturus Retrieval creates the operational precedent for the Blackstar Marshals.

**Year 218:** The Crossroads Massacre. Civilian ships are destroyed while national fleets lose critical time to jurisdiction and authorization disputes.

**Year 219:** The Galactic Defense Force is founded as an independent, border-free naval force.

**Year 222:** The Crossroads Charter establishes the Helion Concordat and formally joins INSA, Gatewatch, and the GDF as three coequal independent arms.

**Year 226:** The Severed Flag Crisis establishes that national governments cannot recall or command Concordat officers serving under Charter oath.

**Year 231:** Operation Open Road reopens pirate-controlled Connector Gate chains through coordinated INSA intelligence, Gatewatch technical support, and GDF fleet action.

**Year 238:** The Three-Key Doctrine is created for selected classified technologies requiring INSA, Gatewatch, and GDF authorization.

**Year 244:** The Black Glass Incident is sealed under Concordat classification.

**Year 252:** The Meridian Relief Crisis establishes the Right of Humanitarian Passage doctrine.

**Year 263-264:** The Silent War targets INSA, Gatewatch, GDF logistics, research, and communications. No public enemy is named.

**Year 271:** The Battle of the Vesper Chain demonstrates the GDF's mature full-spectrum fleet doctrine.

**Year 279:** The Verge Signal triggers a classified joint Concordat operation.

**Year 250-287:** Present era. Civilization recovered. New mysteries gather around the Gates, the frontier, and Unknown Space.

**Year 287:** Game begins. Kepler system. Player starts.

## 44. THE SEVERANCE

### THE EVENT

**Year 187 AS, Day 47**

At 03:47:22 standard time, the Helion Gate network began failing.

It was not a single event. It was a cascade.

**The Cascade:**
- Gate 47 (Kepler) failed first
- Within 6 hours, 143 gates failed
- Within 72 hours, 891 gates failed
- Within 30 days, 1,247 gates failed
- Within 6 months, 1,891 gates failed (total)

### WHAT CAUSED THE SEVERANCE?

**Public Knowledge:** Catastrophic failure of unknown origin.

**Commonwealth Official Position:** A natural disaster.

**Helix Classified:** Gate failures were preceded by unexplained signals and anomalous readings.

**Directorate Official Position:** Proves centralized authority is necessary.

**Compact Official Position:** Proves the Core abandoned the frontier.

**Vael Knowledge:** Resembles something from their history. They recognize the characteristics but have not explained.

**Khepri Knowledge:** Unknown. Khepri activity increased after the Severance.

**Author Truth:** UNDECIDED.

## CONCLUSION

Helion is a universe of mystery, opportunity, and conflict. It is a place where humanity has inherited a crime scene—a network of ancient gates built by unknown hands for unknown purposes. It is a place where corporations, governments, criminals, and aliens compete for resources, knowledge, and survival. It is a place where the player can be anything: miner, trader, explorer, pirate, soldier, scientist, diplomat, or something else entirely.

The lore is designed to generate gameplay. Every faction, every mystery, every technology exists to create opportunities for players to make choices, take risks, and shape their own stories within a living universe.

The central mystery—the Helion Gate network itself—remains unresolved. Humanity did not build it. The Vael did not build it. The Choir may understand part of it. The Null may be trying to contain it. And humanity has been turning it back on.

What happens next is up to the players.
